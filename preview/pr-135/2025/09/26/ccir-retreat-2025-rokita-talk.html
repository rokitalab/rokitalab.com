From Foundation to Future: Dr. Rokita presents the first year of the BTI Bioinformatics Core

jo lynne rokita September 26, 2025 September 08, 2026
team

At this year’s Center for Cancer and Immunology Research (CCIR) Retreat, Dr. Rokita gave a talk, entitled, “From Foundation to Future: The First Year of the BTI Bioinformatics Core.”

Her presentation reflected on the Core’s earliest months — how the team has grown, the rising demand for our support across the BTI and CCIR, and our vision for what comes next.

Thank you to everyone who has been part of building the Core so far, and to CCIR for the chance to share our story!
