Congratulations to [Dr. Kaylyn Clark](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/05-kaylyn-clark.html) on being selected for the [TEAMS](https://www.urmc.rochester.edu/teams-program) training program.

TEAMS (Together Everyone Achieves More in Science) is an NIH-funded national training initiative led by the University of Rochester, Children’s National Research Institute, and The George Washington University. 
The program is designed to equip early-career biomedical researchers with the skills needed to build, lead, and sustain high-performing research teams. 

Over a 10-month structured experience, participants engage in a combination of in-person leadership workshops, virtual coursework, and peer mentorship to develop expertise in leadership, team science, communication, and collaborative research management.

Congratulations, Kaylyn!
