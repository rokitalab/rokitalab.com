So grateful for 1.5 days of dedicated Rokita Lab time this week with our amazing scientist team — [Dr. Ryan Corbett](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/02-ryan-corbett.html), [Dr. Patricia Sullivan](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/03-patricia-sullivan.html), [Dr. Meg Goswami](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/04-meg-goswami.html), and [Dr. Chao Di](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/10-chao-di.html).

It was our very first dedicated lab retreat, and we made the most of every minute: diving into our many active projects, planning new experiments, strategizing on current and future grants, and envisioning each manuscript ahead.

I'm walking away energized and thankful for a team that brings so much talent, curiosity, creativity, and heart to our mission of improving outcomes for children with brain tumors. 
The best ideas happen when we give ourselves the time to think. ✨
