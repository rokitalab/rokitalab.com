CONGRATULATIONS to our Staff Scientist, [Dr. Meghali Goswami](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/04-meg-goswami.html), for winning **first place poster** at the [2026 Virginia Tech Fralin Biomedical Research Institute Cancer Research Center Symposium](https://fbri.vtc.vt.edu/events/special-events/crc-symposium.html)! 🏆

The symposium was held November 9-10 in Alexandria, Virginia, bringing together investigators, clinicians, trainees, and students to share ideas and spark new collaborations across neuro-oncology, cancer immunology, myeloid malignancies, and discovery science.

This caps off an incredible run for the Rokita Lab, following our wins at the [GW Bioinformatics Symposium]({% post_url 2026-10-23-gwu-bioinformatics-symposium %}) and Meg's first place abstract at the [CHARM symposium]({% post_url 2026-10-28-cgt-symposium-first-place %})!
So proud of you, Meg! ✨
