What a day at the [GW Bioinformatics Symposium](https://publichealth.gwu.edu/gw-bioinformatics-symposium)! 🎉

Two of our George Washington University master's students, [Vinay Gujjula](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/08-vinay-gujjula.html) and [Jacob White](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/07-jacob-white.html), presented posters, and [Dr. Kaylyn Clark](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/05-kaylyn-clark.html) presented as our postdoc.
Congratulations to Vinay on winning **first place** and to Jacob on winning **second place** in the graduate student poster competition! 🏆

Dr. Rokita also gave a talk on [TAPESTRY](https://tapestry.rokitalab.com), our interactive resource for exploring tumor-enriched splice junctions in pediatric brain tumors.

Thank you to the symposium organizers, and to everyone who stopped by the posters for the thoughtful questions and conversations.
So proud of this team! ✨
