We are excited to share our preprint, "TAPESTRY: an interactive resource for exploring tumor-enriched splice junctions in pediatric brain tumors," now on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31816)!
This work was spearheaded by [Patricia Sullivan](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/03-patricia-sullivan.html), with underlying data generated by [Dr. Ryan Corbett](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/02-ryan-corbett.html) and infrastructure support by [Chao Di](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/21-chao-di.html).

TAPESTRY currently catalogs >45,000 recurrent tumor-enriched splice junctions across 19 histologies from 1,962 RNA-seq specimens, compared against four normal brain reference cohorts.
Junctions are classified as tumor-specific or oncofetal and annotated for splice event type, coding consequence, novelty, and protein domain overlap.

The web app supports search, filtering, expression visualizations, and downloads, and it includes a REST API.
We hope it helps prioritize splice-derived targets for antibody-based, cell-based, and vaccine immunotherapies.
Try it at [tapestry.rokitalab.com](https://tapestry.rokitalab.com)!
