So proud of [Dr. Ryan Corbett](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/02-ryan-corbett.html) and [Dr. Meg Goswami](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/04-meg-goswami.html), who represented the Rokita Lab at the 2026 American Association for Cancer Research (AACR) Pediatric Cancer Conference! 🎉

They presented two posters.
The first describes how tumor-specific splicing can nominate new therapeutic targets for pediatric brain tumors.
The second introduces our web app, which lets researchers explore this splicing landscape without writing any code.
You can try it yourself at [tapestry.rokitalab.com](https://tapestry.rokitalab.com).

Thank you to everyone who stopped by for the thoughtful questions and conversations.
Thank you also to all of our co-authors, whose work made these posters possible.
Here's to renewed inspiration and new collaborations in the fight against pediatric brain cancer. ✨
