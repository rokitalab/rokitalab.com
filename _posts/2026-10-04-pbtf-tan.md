Dr. Rokita has joined the [Pediatric Brain Tumor Foundation (PBTF)](https://www.curethekids.org/) Technology Advisory Network (TAN)!

The TAN brings together leaders in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, and digital health to help accelerate the future of pediatric brain tumor research.
As PBTF advances its vision of technology-enabled translational research, TAN advisors guide the development of data infrastructure, AI-driven discovery, and innovative digital tools that help turn complex biomedical data into actionable insights for researchers and clinicians.

Dr. Rokita is honored to serve among such a distinguished group of experts in AI, machine learning, data science, and digital health.
We are honored to volunteer our expertise in genomics and computational biology in support of PBTF's mission to advance research for children with brain tumors.
