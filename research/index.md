### Tool and Resource Development
{:.divider}

We develop open source resources that can drive innovation and accelerate the discovery of novel therapeutic strategies for pediatric brain tumor research.
Our newest tool, [TAPESTRY](https://tapestry.rokitalab.com/), was developed by [Dr. Patricia Sullivan](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/03-patricia-sullivan.html), with tumor-enriched junction data generated by [Dr. Ryan Corbett](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/02-ryan-corbett.html) and AWS infrastructure support from [Dr. Chao Di](https://rokitalab.com/labmembers/10-chao-di.html).

### Integrative Multi-omics Approach
{:.divider}

Our research harnesses the power of integrative multi-omics to unravel the complexities of pediatric brain tumors.
By combining genomics, transcriptomics, and proteomics, we investigate how RNA splicing drives tumor formation and progression, and prioritize splice-derived peptides as candidate tumor-specific vulnerabilities for personalized immunotherapy.
In collaboration with [Dr. Sharon Diskin](https://diskinlab.org/), we also characterize patient and parental germlines to pinpoint pathogenic variants that predispose patients to brain tumor development.

### Clinical Trial Support
{:.divider}

We bring real-time bioinformatics to the clinic, analyzing patient tumor data to help guide personalized treatment strategies for children with brain tumors.

**IMPACT**: [Immunotherapy for Malignant Pediatric Brain Tumors Employing Adoptive Cellular Therapy (IMPACT)](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06193759) is a phase 1 interventional study led by [Dr. Brian R. Rood](https://research.childrensnational.org/people/brian-rood).
It tests personalized tumor-specific antigen T cells (TSA-T), which target proteogenomically determined antigens from each patient's own tumor, in children under 5 years of age with embryonal brain tumors.
As a sub-investigator, Dr. Rokita led a team of bioinformatics scientists and engineers in developing and implementing the IMPACT bioinformatics workflows, which are available on [GitHub](https://github.com/childrens-bti).

**SUPERNOVA-RBT**: [SUPERNOVA-RBT](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07851636) is a pilot interventional study led by [Dr. Adriana Fonseca](https://research.childrensnational.org/people/fonseca-adriana) that tests real-time drug screening and genomic sequencing of tumor tissue taken during surgery, in children and young adults with relapsed ultra-rare brain tumors.
A specialized tumor board uses the results to assign a personalized treatment plan.
As a sub-investigator, Dr. Rokita oversaw the bioinformatic integration of genomic, transcriptomic, and drug-screen data, which places each tumor's response to therapy in its molecular context.

### Health Disparities
{:.divider}

We are deeply passionate about exploring the impact of social determinants of health, race, ethnicity, and genetic-based ancestry on pediatric brain tumors.
In collaboration with [Dr. Cassie Kline](https://www.chop.edu/doctors/kline-cassie), we identified significant associations between genetic ancestry superpopulations, CNS tumor histologies and molecular subtypes, patient survival, and upfront treatment approaches.
